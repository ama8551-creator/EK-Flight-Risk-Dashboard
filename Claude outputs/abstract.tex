\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

Airline revenue management, or airline inventory management, is crucial in generating income. Seat inventory control and overbooking strategies can yield real income benefits for airlines. Inventory analysts can help by observing flights and taking immediate action, such as overbooking, to compensate for cancellations, no-shows, or unsold seats.

Currently, analysts mainly rely on static reports that are updated periodically. Such reports do not show real-time booking activity and do not prioritize flights by their urgency. Therefore, analysts may fail to consider the most urgent flights in time, which can be costly and affect operations such as denied boardings and downgrades.

This thesis suggests a real-time decision support dashboard to help analysts detect and prioritize high-risk flights in real-time. A composite risk score will be generated based on multiple features, including time to departure, cabin load factor, forecast seat factor, no-show rate, and cancellation rate. This score will operate in an interactive dashboard developed in Python with Streamlit. The real-time conditions will be simulated through replaying booking activity on a compressed timeline. For this study, a fully synthetic dataset will be generated by the researcher based on parameters and distributions used in airline revenue management literature. The output will be a prototype of the dashboard, which demonstrates faster detection of high-risk flights, reduced manual analysis, and improved operations of inventory analysts.

\vspace{1em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} airline revenue management; airline inventory management; real-time decision support; prioritization dashboard; high-risk flight prioritization; data analytics; synthetic data
